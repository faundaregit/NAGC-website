About NAGC

Experience built across species, systems, and borders

NAGC brings practical conservation science, technology development, and repository thinking together around one goal: making aquatic genetic resources more durable, usable, and responsibly stewarded.

200+Species5+Federal agencies20+Countries30+Years of experience

Our purpose

Build preservation capacity that lasts beyond one project

Aquatic germplasm conservation requires more than a freezing protocol. It depends on biological understanding, repeatable collection and processing, defensible quality control, complete metadata, appropriate storage, and people who can sustain the work.

NAGC helps partners connect those pieces into a practical system.

How we contribute

One team across the preservation lifecycle

Our work spans technical development, implementation, stewardship, and knowledge transfer.

Operating principles

Conservation resources must be trusted to be useful

Quality before volume

Scale matters only when identity, viability, repeatability, and documentation remain defensible.

Fit the biology

Methods should respond to the organism and biological material, not force every project into one template.

Design for adoption

Tools and protocols must work in the facilities, field settings, and staffing realities where they will be used.

Plan for stewardship

Ownership, access, metadata, storage, and future use should be addressed before material enters a repository.

A practical conservation partner

From a biological sample to a resource that can endure

We are building the technical capacity, repeatable workflows, and partnerships needed to responsibly safeguard aquatic germplasm. Each engagement begins with the species, the conservation goal, and a clear plan for quality control.

Service availability, timelines, and pricing are confirmed during consultation.

Scalable workflows

Methods and tools designed to move conservation programs from one-off efforts toward repeatable practice.

03

Knowledge transfer

Training and consultation that help partners build durable capacity in their own facilities.

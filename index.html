Who We Are

Loading laboratory image…

The National Aquatic Germplasm Center (NAGC) is a pioneering institution dedicated to safeguarding the genetic heritage of aquatic species across North America. Through cutting-edge cryopreservation technology and collaborative research partnerships, we maintain a living genetic library that ensures the resilience of aquatic biodiversity for generations to come.

Founded on the principle that genetic diversity is the cornerstone of species survival, NAGC works at the intersection of reproductive biology, genomics, and conservation science. Our state-of-the-art facilities house the nation's most comprehensive collection of aquatic germplasm specimens.

Through strategic partnerships with federal agencies, universities, tribal nations, and private hatcheries, we have built an unparalleled network dedicated to the preservation and restoration of imperiled aquatic species, from iconic salmon runs to ancient sturgeon populations.

Our Programs

🧬

Cryopreservation Program

Our flagship program applies state-of-the-art freezing protocols to preserve the genetic material of at-risk aquatic species. We maintain long-term repositories ensuring biodiversity is available for future restoration efforts.

🔬

Research & Innovation

Pushing the boundaries of reproductive science, our research teams develop novel cryoprotectants, assess sperm quality through advanced motility analysis, and pioneer gene-banking methodologies.

🤝

Partnership & Outreach

We collaborate with federal agencies, tribal nations, universities, and private hatcheries to build a national network of aquatic genetic resource stewardship and shared knowledge.

🌱

Education & Training

Through hands-on workshops and training programs, we equip the next generation of aquatic scientists with practical cryopreservation skills and conservation biology expertise.

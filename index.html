Our mission

A national-scale resource for aquatic genetic preservation

NAGC safeguards aquatic genetic resources through cryopreservation services and repository development. We are building a future in which the legacy of every aquatic species can be supported by sustainable, accessible germplasm banking.

Preservation pathway

A disciplined route from project definition to stewardship

01

Define

Clarify the species, biological material, conservation goal, scale, and decision criteria.

02

Develop

Select or adapt collection, processing, cryopreservation, and quality-control methods.

03

Validate

Document performance, repeatability, metadata, and operational requirements.

04

Steward

Plan storage, verification, access, training, and long-term program continuity.
